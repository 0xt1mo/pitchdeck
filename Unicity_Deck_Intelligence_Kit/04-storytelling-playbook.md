# 04 · Storytelling Playbook

From Alan's Pip Decks training (Storyteller + Idea Tactics). These shape **structure and hooks** — not register. Voice (01) still governs the words.

## Frame the stakes (open here)
- **Dragon & City** — frame as an epic: the *City* = the status quo (valuable but unfair/wasteful); the *Dragon* = the threat that also hoards the opportunity. (Here: City = today's payment system; Dragon = unverifiable machine money + the hoarded opportunity of the agentic economy.) **Never let this framing leak into slide copy** — it structures thinking only.
- **Three Great Conflicts** — pick the ONE conflict at the heart (vs Nature / Society / Self). Contrast Dream vs Nightmare.
- **Hero & Guide** — the **audience is the hero**, you are the Guide (Yoda, not Luke). Greg / the institution is the hero; Unicity is the tool you hand them. Stop talking about yourself.

## The arc (this IS the 70/30 "build the problem" instinct)
- **POPP:** Problem (−) → Opportunity (+) → Practical steps (−, "won't be easy") → Promise (+). Alternate down/up for drama.
- Spend real time in the **hole** (the problem) before the climb. Man-in-a-hole / Rags-to-riches rhythm.
- **Pride & Fall** — the sceptic's arc; the perfect lens for the *incumbents* slide (their flaw = working around the ledger they built).

## Hooks (open loops that pull slide → slide)
- **Secrets & Puzzles** — open an information gap (a secret you hold, or an anomaly/paradox) — and always pay it off. (e.g. open on "nothing vouches for them" → pay off at the gap + receive predicate.)
- **Leave it Out** — omit, hint, let the audience complete it.

## Craft (sentence / slide level)
- **Movie Time** — every beat should play a picture in the head (Action → Emotion → Meaning). If no picture plays, it's data, not story.
- **Rolls-Royce Moment** — one vivid exemplary detail that stands for the whole.
- **Three is the Magic Number** — limit takeaways to three; reversal-of-three; attention-of-three.
- **Data Detectives** — wrap every number in a story; numbers are remembered as stories.
- **Make a Meme** — distil the whole thing to one stupidly-simple, profoundly-true line. (Ours: *"trust has to travel with the money."*)
- **Peak-End** — engineer one unmistakable peak (the x402 "twelve steps become five") and a deliberate closing mood (the "walled garden / own the layer" close).
- **Human Truths** — anchor to universal needs (autonomy, safety, belonging, progress); the magic lives in the tensions.

## Pressure-test (SICFAM / ProvocaPrompts)
"Why should anyone care?" · "What makes this impossible to copy?" · Is it Simple, Interesting, Coherent, Feasible, Audience-appropriate, Memorable?
