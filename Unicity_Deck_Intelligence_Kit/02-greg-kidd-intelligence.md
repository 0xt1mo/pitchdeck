# 02 · The Audience — Greg Kidd & Hard Yaka

You cannot write this deck well without understanding him. He is not "an investor." He has spent 40 years assembling one idea — **fair access to money, identity, and communications** — from every angle.

## Who he is
- **Career:** Booz Allen → co-founded DMSC (NASDAQ) → **Fed payments division** → **Promontory** (regulatory consulting) → **Ripple Chief Risk Officer** (early investor, *not* founder) → **founded GlobaliD** (self-sovereign identity) → now **Chairman/CEO of USBC**.
- **Hard Yaka** (2011, with Jun Hiraga): 250+ companies; 7 unicorns / 7 IPOs / 27 acquisitions. Early money in **Twitter, Square, Coinbase, Robinhood, Solana**. (Say "early investor," never "first money" — he knows his own cap table.)
- **He reads compliance *architecture*, not pitches.** He was literally the risk officer. This governs everything below.

## His doctrine (echo his own language back to him)
- **"Identity is the permission to act."** (used verbatim on the gap slide)
- **Fair access** — "everyone deserves a seat at the table for their money." Identity is "a right and a responsibility."
- **Run toward the smoke** — *"manage risk, don't avoid it; have the bad actors on the platform rather than the Sisyphean effort to keep them all out."* (verbatim-confirmed)
- **Attest the existence and compliance of required data — not the private data behind it.**

## ⭐ The biggest, most current intelligence (verified June 2026)
- **USBC is LIVE.** Oct 2025, with **Uphold + Vast Bank**, USBC launched the *"world's first retail tokenized U.S. dollar deposits"* with worldwide access — bank-issued, FDIC-eligible, Reg-E protected, on a **compliant/permissioned chain**, explicitly positioned *against* stablecoins. MVP pillars: Send · Spend · Convert · Load/Unload · Recover.
- **USBC already does "Proof of Personhood"** via biometrics, *"in a privacy-preserving way that lets users keep control of their data."* → He is already shipping identity-gated, privacy-preserving compliant money. **Unicity's Receive Predicate is the protocol-level version of what USBC hand-builds.**
- **"Know Your Agent" (KYA)** is the live 2026 frame — the agent counterpart to KYC. *"You can't let autonomous agents pay unless you know who they are, what authority they have, and who they represent."* This is the bridge from his identity thesis to the agentic economy — and exactly what the Receive Predicate enforces.

## The strategic consequence (how to pitch HIM)
Do **not** pitch Unicity as a cool protocol. Pitch it as the **settlement substrate that lets his own vision (USBC) run peer-to-peer, private, at machine speed — without the permissioned-chain compromise — and extends it to agents via KYA.** The "regulated deposit issuer" in the close *is him*. He should feel Unicity finishes the sentence he's been writing for a decade.

## ✅ What makes him lean in
- Compliance-as-architecture (enforced by the asset, not a venue).
- Privacy that is still attestable to the one counterparty/regulator who must be satisfied.
- Fair access framed as the through-line of his portfolio (Ripple · GlobaliD · USBC).
- A first-conversation ask with **no money ask** — relationship-first.

## 🔴 What turns him off instantly (he's a risk officer — these lose the room)
- **Name-drops he can't verify.** "A DAC *for* BlackRock" → he asks "is BlackRock a client?" → the walk-back poisons every legitimate claim. (Fixed to "a weather-based parametric insurer that runs entirely on the protocol.")
- **Overclaims / superlatives:** "first to solve the trilemma," "the only protocol," "unlimited," "guarantees collapse." A CRO files these under hype on sight.
- **Imprecise verbs:** "forces the proof to travel natively" reads as a hack. Use "binds … so it travels with every payment."
- **Hype, jargon, and the banned constructions** (see 01).

## The portfolio slide (Option B) — humility, not dominance
Earlier draft over-reached ("Unicity is the layer beneath them all / the protocol that finishes the job") — a titanic claim. Reframed to **fit**: *"Identity and compliant value on open infrastructure — the thesis behind Ripple, GlobaliD, and USBC. Unicity shares it, focused on one frontier: trust between machines."* We belong in his thesis; we don't tower over it.
