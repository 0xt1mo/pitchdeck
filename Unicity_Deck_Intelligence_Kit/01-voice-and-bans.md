# 01 · Voice & Copy DNA (0xt1mo)

The copywriter is **0xt1mo**. The standard isn't "is this good copy" — it's *"does this read like he wrote it."*
Source of truth for his real lines: `~/pitchdeck/deck-content.md` (his Series A deck) and `~/pitchdeck/0xt1mo-voice-guide.md`.

## The register
Restrained, understated, systems-precise. Tighter than a normal investor deck. **No hype.** One specific claim per line. Concrete over abstract. He earns the reader's belief with precision, not adjectives.

## Signature moves (use these — they're his)
- **Historical inflection:** *"Satoshi's whitepaper was titled 'P2P electronic cash.' 17 years later we have neither."* / *"The first web connected people. The next one is connecting AI agents."*
- **Category by negation (his, not the banned kind):** *"Shared ledgers were designed for humans — slow, sequential, contested. Agents don't work that way."* / *"Machines don't transact the way we do."*
- **Architectural refusal (mantra-like, repeatable):** *"No shared ledger. No bottlenecks."* / *"Remove the shared ledger entirely."*
- **Proof by essentials:** *"the chain only prevents double-spending"* / *"Everyone validating everything cannot scale."* / *"validation at the edge, not a set of validators."*
- **The message is the payment.** (x402) · **Each token is itself a blockchain.** · **Machine finance needs a different primitive.**
- **Concrete triples:** *"unlimited throughput, one-second finality, a microcent per transaction."*
- **Endgame close:** restate the capability → name the upside → one category-owning line. *"Put it inside the dollar, and you own the layer the agentic economy settles on."*

## Cadence & punctuation
- Short declarative lead (< 20 words), then unpack.
- Parallel fragments: *"A CEX gives you speed and takes custody. A DEX gives you custody and takes privacy. Unicity gives you all four."*
- **Em-dashes for expansion — never semicolons in body copy.** (Caught two semicolons in the architecture slide; converted to em-dashes.)
- All-caps is for concepts, never for emphasis/hype. No exclamation marks.

## Headlines = declarative claims, NOT "Label: payoff"
We converted every "The structural bottleneck: the shared ledger" → a claim that lands on its own, with orange on the key phrase:
- THE COUNTERPARTY IS NOW A MACHINE · MACHINES CANNOT WAIT ON A SHARED LEDGER · EVERY STABLECOIN PICKS TWO · IDENTITY WAS NEVER BUILT IN · THE NETWORK ATTESTS ONE THING · THE CHAIN DOES ONE JOB · THE ASSET ENFORCES ITS OWN COMPLIANCE · PRIVATE AGAINST EVERY OBSERVER · SETTLEMENT WITH NO INTERMEDIARY · TWELVE STEPS BECOME FIVE · A MARKET WITH NO VENUE · A COMPANY RUN BY AGENTS.
- The headline *states the thing*; the body *proves it*.

## Vocabulary
**Use:** on-chain/off-chain · verifiable / cryptographic / tamper-evident · peer-to-peer / bearer / self-proving · settles / settlement · throughput / finality / latency · identity / permission / authority / counterparty · the asset / inside the asset.
**Avoid:** revolutionary, disruptive, breakthrough, powerful, seamless, unlock, empower, leverage, synergy; adjective stacks; "next-gen"; gerund openers where a verb is stronger.

## 🚫 HARD BANS (Alan, repeated + emphatic — strip on sight, every deck/page/line)
1. **The word "rail" / "rails"** — never. Not "payment rails," "open rails," "the rails." Say: the network, the system, the layer, the wires, the infrastructure — or rephrase. (*"audit trail"* is a different word and is fine.)
2. **"This is not X, this is Y" rhetoric** and its kin: *"not just X but Y," "X, not Y," "proven, not promised," "designed out, not patched."* State the positive directly; drop the negated foil. (Note: 0xt1mo's *authentic* negation — "Agents don't work that way," "we have neither" — is a plain declarative truth and is fine. The ban is the *marketing-foil* pattern.)
3. **Jargon / framing leakage** — strip gratuitous jargon, and NEVER let internal framing devices (the "Dragon & City" spine, etc.) appear in slide copy. The spine structures *my thinking*, not the audience's view.
4. **No hype words, no exclamation marks, no superlatives a risk officer discounts** ("first to solve the trilemma," "the only protocol," "unlimited"). See 03.

## Lines we killed (and why) — pattern library of what NOT to do
- *"The machine economy cannot run on human infrastructure."* → generic AI-infra filler. Became *"When the counterparty is a machine, trust has to travel with the money."*
- *"machine commerce requires machine trust"* → tautological buzzword. Became *"the counterparty is now a machine."*
- *"They pay each other faster than anything built for people can stop to ask…"* → tortured, unreadable. Became *"Machines now pay each other directly — and nothing stops to ask who they are, or whether they should."*
- *"Now applying that to the rails your agents transact on"* → banned word. Became *"…to the money your agents move."*
- *"the first protocol to solve all three"* → overclaim. Became *"holds all three."*

## Field-tested elevations (from the Padra deep-audit — apply these)
A 5-lens voice audit against his real corpus surfaced the moves that separate "competent agency copy" from *him*:
1. **Lead on the world-fact; never bury the inflection.** Don't open a slide on a soft trend-noun ("the patient journey is going agentic"). State the shift as a flat fact about the world — *"The clinic now runs before the patient arrives."* The sharpest beat goes FIRST, not stranded at the end of a paragraph.
2. **The thing IS the boundary (architectural refusal), not a guardrail handed over.** *"The Policy Engine is the boundary the agent cannot cross"* beats *"gives every agent a boundary."* Control lives beneath the agent and enforces; it isn't "given."
3. **Use his real signature lines verbatim when they fit.** *"You cannot prompt-inject past a kernel boundary."* / *"Agents run on top of it."* / *"The message is the payment."* — reach for the actual line, not a paraphrase.
4. **His authentic declarative negation is allowed; the marketing foil is not.** Keep *"agents don't work that way"*, *"stop answering and start acting"*. Drop *"X, not Y"* / *"not just X"* — **even his own** *"below the agent, not beside it"* gets dropped per Alan's hard ban. Express the kernel idea without the foil (*"runs beneath the agent"*).
5. **Bookend the spine verbatim.** Repeat the one mantra line at the cover and the close, word-for-word (*"Permission before every action. Proof after every one."*) — Peak-End + his repeated-mantra habit.
6. **State the proven fact; drop the foil that sets it up.** *"is not a startup learning the problem today"* → just *"The platform is already built. … in production since 2012."* Let the fact carry; don't prop it with a negated strawman.
