# 03 · Fact-Discipline

Greg is a Fed / Ripple-CRO reader. A single loose number or overclaim is a "tell" that lets him discount the whole deck. These are the rules that keep every claim defensible.

## The numbers — exact framing
| Claim | Correct framing | Never |
|---|---|---|
| **57.5%** | of **automated web traffic / requests** (Cloudflare, 2026) | not "of transactions," not on-chain |
| **30,000 tx/sec** | per **shard**, **by design** (Unicity architectural target) | not "live/benchmarked," not "unlimited," not "300M" |
| **300,000 tx/sec** | **Guardtime/KSI heritage** — Eesti Pank's 2021 central-bank test (the team's prior architecture) | NEVER as a live Unicity figure; never "the ECB tested Unicity" |
| **The three papers** | prove **privacy** + **no-double-spend** only — public on github.com/unicitynetwork | never attribute throughput/TPS to the papers |
| **x402** | ~100M txns on Base in ~9 months (use the tx count) | not economic volume, not agent counts |
| **Circle / Solana / AP2·x402** | real competitor facts (Arc L1, $222M presale, Malachite; Alpenglow ~150ms) | don't conflate the $222M with the Malachite price |

## Naming & claims
- **BlackRock DAC** = a **reference build** Unicity made (a weather-based parametric insurer that runs on the protocol). NEVER "a DAC *for* BlackRock" / implying a client engagement. If BlackRock is ever a nameable client, only then name it.
- **No "Plasma"** naming anywhere.
- **eIDAS-aligned**, not "accredited."
- Greg **founded GlobaliD**; was Ripple **early investor + CRO** — keep distinct.
- **USBC = "tokenized dollar deposit"** (his distinction from stablecoins). Don't characterize its consensus model; stay non-triumphant about it.
- Team heritage (NATO/DARPA/Lockheed/Verizon/Maersk) is the **founders' Guardtime work** — frame as lineage, not "Unicity's customers."

## Banned overclaims (a CRO's allergy)
- "the **first** protocol to solve the trilemma" → "holds all three"
- "the **only** protocol delivering…" → "Unicity delivers…"
- "**guarantees** network collapse" → "you hit the ceiling — on scale and on privacy"
- "**unlimited** throughput" → "30,000 tx/sec per shard, by design"
- Absolutes like "Absolute confidentiality" → "the floor for institutional capital"

## The principle
When in doubt, **scope the claim and attribute the source.** A smaller, precise, sourced claim beats a big asserted one — especially for this audience.
