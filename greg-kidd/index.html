07 · THE PRIMITIVE · THE RECEIVE PREDICATE

Compliance the asset enforces on itself.

A receive predicate is a rule the bearer asset carries and applies to its own transfer — inside the money, not beside it.
The predicate checks a self-contained attestation in-band — no issuer call at settlement time.
Revocation rides in the attestation: short-lived attestations, accumulator proofs. Stale credential, no settlement. Freshness is the issuer's cadence, not the settlement's bottleneck.
The credential isn't checked at the door. It is the door.

09 · THE COORDINATION PROOF · NO CALLBACK

Both parties commit. Nobody chases a timeout.

Hashed Timelock (HTLC)
A locks & reveals a hash · B locks against it · A claims, revealing the preimage · B must claim before the timeout.
THE TIMING TRAP — B is late on the last step, a dropped connection, and A keeps both.
Unicity Predicate Swap
A commits & B commits — independently — against one shared reference.
Both locked → the swap completes. Either walked away → both refunded. No deadlines, no chasing.
The same structure answers the freshness question — verification is self-contained and in-band; no issuer to call back, no clock to lose.

12 · THE BOUNDARY · WHAT A CHARTER CANNOT CROSS

A charter settles for its members.
The space between charters has to be built.

The strongest version of the argument
“A permissioned chain on one bank charter already puts identity inside the money — USBC proves it works. Why generalize?”
1USBC proves the conclusion: identity belongs inside the money. It works.
2A charter settles for the parties on that charter. Value can't round-trip to a counterparty banking somewhere else without a trusted middle — the boundary a charter cannot cross by construction.
3An agent at machine speed cannot phone any single charter's verifier in-band. Cross-charter, cross-agent settlement is the one thing a charter structurally can't do — no matter how good the charter.
Unicity is the rail that lets a USBC deposit settle to a counterparty on a different charter. USBC reached it on one charter; this generalizes the move — it doesn't replace it.
