The operating system for autonomous AI.

A multi-tenant OS that runs agent fleets at data-center scale — with a cryptographic proof of every execution. Verifiable, secure by construction.

What we invented

AOS runs thousands of agents per node on a shared, secure kernel — 1000× the density of containers, 50× faster launch time, and millisecond latency to first token. Beneath it, a cryptographic proof system records every input, action and output into a content-addressable, verifiable provenance graph.

Why it matters

Agents are speedrunning compute history — from laptops to rented boxes to data centers. They are becoming a data-center workload with no safe OS to run on; bolt-on guardrails break at scale. AOS enforces policy and audit below the agent, clearing EU AI Act, SOC 2, HIPAA and sovereign requirements by construction. Agents cannot go rogue.
